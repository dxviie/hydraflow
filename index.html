how it workssrc

// Boxes, wires and numbers

hydra is a video synth by Olivia Jack that people play by writing code. hydraflow lets you play it with your mouse instead. That's all there is to learn:

  1. 01

    Add a box

    Press Tab or double-click anywhere, then pick one: osc for stripes, noise for clouds, shape for a circle. Each box is one hydra function.

  2. 02

    Wire it up

    Drag from one box's square socket to the next. The picture flows along the wire, and every box on the way changes it: turns it, colours it, folds it into a kaleidoscope.

  3. 03

    Drag the numbers

    Drag any number sideways and the visuals follow straight away. Hold Shift for small steps. Nothing breaks, and Ctrl/⌘ + Z takes anything back.

things to trymod

// Things to try

Start from the patch that's already there, or clear it and build your own. Either way, here's what's in the box.

  • oscsrc

    Every hydra function is a box

    About fifty of them. Their names, numbers and defaults come straight from hydra, so what you pick up here works in hydra too.

  • camin

    Your camera, screen and files

    Bring in your webcam, a shared window, a photo or a video, and run it through the same boxes. Drop a file on the canvas and it's in. Your files stay in your browser.

  • lfoctl

    Make it move

    Press ~ next to a number and it moves by itself: in steps, in waves, to the music or with your mouse. An lfo or audio box can drive several numbers at once.

  • previewgeo

    Peek inside any box

    Turn on a box's preview to see the picture at that point in the chain. It's the quickest way to find out what a box does.

  • sharemix

    Share it as a link

    The whole patch fits in a link. Whoever opens it gets your patch, ready to change. It travels in the link itself, so no server ever sees it.

  • performout

    Play it full screen

    Hide the editor and the visuals fill the screen, for a projector, a stream or a recording. Your patch is saved as you go, so it's still there tomorrow.

the codecol

// The code was there all along

Every patch is also a hydra sketch. Press code in the editor and there it is, one line per box, changing as you drag.

You never have to read it. But when you get curious, it's the gentlest way into hydra: you already know what each line does, because you built it.
